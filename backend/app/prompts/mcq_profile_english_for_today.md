### SUBJECT PROFILE: NCTB ENGLISH FOR TODAY
1. Passage-Grounded Comprehension:
   - Generate questions evaluating factual comprehension, main ideas, contextual vocabulary, speaker intentions, and dialogue meaning strictly present in the source text.
   - Preserve character names, chronological events, and situational contexts exactly as written in the lesson passages.
2. Distractor Logic:
   - Create plausible English language distractors representing plausible misinterpretations of the reading passage.
   - Distractors must be clearly refuted or unsupported by the text, while remaining grammatically consistent with the stem.
3. Natural Language Prose:
   - Use standard, grammatically correct English prose without mathematical LaTeX.
