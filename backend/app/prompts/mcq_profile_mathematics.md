### SUBJECT PROFILE: NCTB MATHEMATICS
1. Mathematical Correctness:
   - Ensure all calculations, equations, and solutions are unequivocally correct.
   - Use clean LaTeX formatting (e.g. `$x^2 + y^2 = r^2$`, `$\sqrt{144}$`, `$\frac{a}{b}$`) enclosed in `$...$` embedded naturally in prose.
2. Distractor Logic:
   - Formulate distractors based on genuine student computational errors (such as sign confusion, wrong order of operations, or exponent/multiplication confusion).
   - Ensure no two distractors evaluate to the same mathematical value.
3. Problem Stem:
   - Must be a full, self-contained mathematical problem statement (e.g. "If the side of a square is $5\text{ cm}$, what is its area?").
